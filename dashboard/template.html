What counts as “pressure”?

Pressure balls are the 20% most match-deciding balls: tight chases (required rate against balls and wickets left) and collapses or runaway starts in the first innings. Super overs always count. Stats come from completed matches from 2022: IPL = IPL seasons, International = T20Is. SR = strike rate for batters, balls per wicket for bowlers. Rank orders players by our pressure model within the current filters (it uses all matches); “–” means too little pressure data.
