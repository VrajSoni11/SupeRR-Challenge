What counts as “pressure”?

Pressure balls are the 20% most match-deciding balls: tight chases (the required rate against balls and wickets left) and collapses or runaway starts in the first innings. Super overs always count. All figures come from completed IPL and T20I matches from 2022. SR = strike rate (batters) or balls per wicket (bowlers). Rank orders players by our pressure model within the current filters; “–” means too little pressure data.
